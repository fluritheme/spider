# Fluri package licensing

Current and new theme packages use the Fluri Themes Commercial License in LICENSE. Read LICENSING.md and licensing.json before creating packages, editing licensing or publishing. Use package.json license "SEE LICENSE IN LICENSE" and homepage https://fluri.xyz/. Preserve LICENSE-MIT-LEGACY and third-party notices. Do not replace already-published MIT archives or retag historical releases. Do not label commercial releases MIT or open source. Do not add runtime activation, purchase tracking or watermarks. Do not create a fake checkout or claim payment/customer-access features are live without implementing and verifying them.

New themes inherit this licensing policy unless the user explicitly chooses a different license.
