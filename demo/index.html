THE FINE THREADS.

A good guest.

Will he get in the way?

The decorative canvas ignores pointer events and stays out of the accessibility tree. Links, text selection, and scrolling keep working. Webs stay in place when clicked; remove them explicitly with the Clear silk button or the API.

Does he work on phones?

Yes. The scene responds to the page layout and touch input. He and the silk stay anchored to the document as you scroll. With reduced motion enabled, he stays still and does not automatically weave nets.

How does the web attach to letters?

Text ranges and a small local font mask approximate the visible edges of letters. The spider visits those attachment points and physically traverses the silk it lays. Finished supports follow ordinary layout changes. Perspective, complex clipping, and glyph shaping have documented limits.

Can I make him my own?

Choose scale, speed, silk density, reach, opacity, resident count, and pointer fear. Pause, resume, or clear the silk. The source and complete options are on GitHub.
